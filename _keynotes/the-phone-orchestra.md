Not only is everyone musical, it turns out that everyone also carries a powerful synth in their pocket. In this session, using SuperSonic and some Phoenix PubSub magic, Sam will unleash the synth in your phones and turn the entire audience into a live coded phone orchestra.
Expect noise, beats and a good amount of joyful chaos.
Bring a charged phone, and please don't turn the volume down!
