Questions your boss might ask

"Can't you just watch the recordings?"

Recordings are included with every ticket. The Informal Space, the tutorial and the conversations with the OTP, Elixir and Gleam teams between sessions aren't.

"Is it only for experts?"

No. Talks like Idiomatic Erlang sit next to deep dives from the Erlang/OTP team, so it works for people new to the BEAM and for teams running it in production.

"How big is it?"

Two days, two tracks plus the community-led Informal Space, 30+ speakers and 300+ attendees. Small enough to actually talk to people.

"Is there a group price?"

Yes: 3+ in-person tickets are €683.31 each and 4+ virtual tickets €157.61 each (incl. VAT). For invoices, write to info@codesync.global.
